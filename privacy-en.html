01In one sentence

GongSi Ledger is a fully offline bookkeeping tool. Every record you enter stays on your own device. The developer cannot see or access it.

02We collect no data

The app requires no registration, no login, no phone number or email, and contains no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking SDKs. Specifically:

  • We collect no account info, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking, no advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • The app has no backend server — your ledger is never uploaded anywhere

03Where your data lives

DataStoredWho can access
Ledger records (amounts, dates, parties, categories)On-device database (app sandbox)Only you
Settings (language, currency symbol, Pro status)On-device preferencesOnly you
Automatic backups and manually exported CSV / JSONLocal files, location chosen by youOnly you

Uninstalling the app deletes its data. If you use iCloud or computer backups, copies are managed entirely by Apple's system backup and remain inaccessible to the developer.

04Purchases

Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. The app never sees or stores your payment details; it only reads the local entitlement status to unlock Pro features. Refunds and billing issues are handled by Apple.

05Export and sharing are user-initiated

CSV workbooks and JSON backups are only generated and shared when you explicitly tap the export action. Files are created locally; where they go is decided entirely by you in the system share sheet. Once you share data with a third party, that party's own privacy policy applies.

06System permissions

The app requests no camera, microphone, location, contacts or health permissions. When importing a backup, the system file picker grants access only to the single file you choose.

07Children's privacy

The app is intended for adult sole proprietors and small business owners. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children (in fact, it collects no information from anyone).

08Changes to this policy

If a future version introduces any network-dependent feature (such as optional cloud sync), this page will be updated before that feature ships, with a prominent notice in the app. The "Last updated" date at the top is the currently effective version.
