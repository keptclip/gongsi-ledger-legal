Getting started

  • Tap the + button to add an entry: amount, date, counterparty, category and ownership (Business / Owner Advance)
  • The list is grouped by month; filter by year and type (All / Expenses / Advances / Main income)
  • Swipe left on any entry to delete — with a confirmation step first
  • Tap the chart button for the annual summary, and export the annual CSV workbook directly

FAQ

Where is my data stored? Will I lose it when switching phones?
Everything stays on the device — nothing is uploaded. Before switching phones, export a JSON backup under "Settings → Local Backup" and import it on the new device under "Settings → Restore Backup". Monthly exports are recommended.
The import said "13 entries imported", but I only see 12?
The backup contains records from all years, while the list shows only the selected year. Switch to the matching year — or "All" — to see everything. No data was lost.
Do I need to buy Pro again on a new phone?
No. Pro is a one-time purchase. On the paywall, tap "Restore Purchases" with the same Apple ID you used to buy.
Can I file taxes directly with the exported CSV workbook?
No. GongSi Ledger organizes data; it is not a tax advisor. The exported file is organized source material — have official filings reviewed by a certified accountant.
Can I change the language or currency symbol?
Yes. Settings offers Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese and German; the currency symbol is configurable. Switching never changes your data.
Can I recover a deleted entry?
In-app deletion is permanent, but the app keeps an automatic backup after every change — see "Settings → Local Backup" to inspect and restore the latest one.
Does the app need the network? Does it upload my ledger?
No network, no upload. Apart from App Store purchase validation, the app makes no network requests at all. See the Privacy Policy.
Found a bug or want a feature?
Email us directly, ideally with: iPhone model, iOS version, app version (bottom of Settings), and reproduction steps. Screenshots help.
